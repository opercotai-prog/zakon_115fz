## 1. SOURCE

**Document**: Федеральный закон от 23 августа 2001 г. N 115-ФЗ "О противодействии легализации (отмыванию) доходов, полученных преступным путём, и финансированию терроризма" (с изменениями и дополнениями)

**Article**: 6.1

**Title**: Обязанности юридического лица по раскрытию информации о своих бенефициарных владельцах

**Edition**: Федеральный закон от 23.06.2016 N 215-ФЗ (с последующими изменениями от 28.04.2023 N 165-ФЗ, от 28.06.2021 N 233-ФЗ, от 28.12.2016 N 471-ФЗ, от 05.12.2022 N 498-ФЗ)

**Analysis Focus**: Points 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 (most complex structures with multiple subjects and deadlines)

## 2. SUBJECTS

### 2.1 Primary Subject
- **Entity**: Юридическое лицо (legal entity)
- **Role**: Obligor — must obtain, update, store, and provide information about beneficial owners
- **Scope**: All legal entities (with exceptions noted in point 2)

### 2.2 Secondary Subjects

| Subject | Role | Reference | Classification |
|---------|------|-----------|-----------------|
| Физические и юридические лица, являющиеся учредителями или участниками | Obligor | п.5 | A |
| Контролирующие лиц (иным образом) | Obligor | п.5 | A |
| Уполномоченный орган | Requester | п.6 | A |
| Налоговые органы | Requester | п.6 | A |
| Федеральный орган исполнительной власти в сфере регистрации некоммерческих организаций | Requester | п.6 | A |
| Правительство Российской Федерации | Rule-setter | п.6 | A |
| Иностранные юридические лица и иностранные структуры без образования юридического лица | Obligor | п.7.1 | A |

### 2.3 COMMENTARY

**Multiple Subjects Pattern** (NEW): Unlike Article 7.1 p.3.1 which had a single subject (nотариус), Article 6.1 demonstrates a complex web of actors:
- Primary obligor (legal entity)
- Secondary obligors (founders, participants, controlling entities)
- Requesters (authorized bodies, tax organs, federal authority)
- Rule-setter (Government establishing procedures)

This is a fundamentally more complex subject structure than previous experiments. Each subject category has a different role and obligation.

**Evidence**: Every point from 1 to 7.1 introduces at least one subject.

**Classification**: A (directly follows from text)

### 4.2 COMPOUND ACTION Analysis

**Point 3.1 — "обновлять информацию ... документально фиксировать"**

Text: "регулярно, но не реже одного раза в год либо в случае изменения сведений обновлять информацию о своих бенефициарных владельцах и документально фиксировать полученную информацию"

**Hypothesis H3 Application** (RECALL: YES, APPLICATION: YES):
- This is a compound action with two verbs: "обновлять" (update) + "документально фиксировать" (record)
- **Common object**: information about beneficial owners
- **Common temporal marker**: "не реже одного раза в год либо в случае изменения сведений"
- **Interpretation**: These are two components of ONE duty, not two independent duties
  - First component: keep information current
  - Second component: document what you have obtained
  - Both are required for proper beneficial owner disclosure

**Classification**: B (follows from structure)

**Result for H3**: CONFIRMED

## 5. OBJECTS

### 5.1 Primary Object

**Main object**: Информация о бенефициарных владельцах (beneficial owner information)

**Definition** (Point 8):
```
физическое лицо, которое в конечном счете прямо или косвенно 
(через третьих лиц) владеет (имеет преобладающее участие более 25 процентов 
в капитале) юридическим лицом либо имеет возможность контролировать его действия
```

### 5.2 Object Segmentation (HYPOTHESIS H1 — OBJECT)

**RECALL: YES | APPLICATION: YES**

The object "информация о своих бенефициарных владельцах" contains multiple semantic layers:

| Layer | Content | Evidence | Type |
|-------|---------|----------|------|
| **Object Head** | информация | "информация о своих бенефициарных владельцах" | Nominal |
| **Qualifier (Type)** | о бенефициарных владельцах | п.1, п.3, п.6 | Semantic qualifier |
| **Scope** | "своих" (possessive) | "информация о своих бенефициарных владельцах" | Possessive qualifier |
| **Secondary object (in п.6)** | документально подтвержденная информация | "имеющуюся документально подтвержденную информацию" | Quality qualifier |
| **Associated content** | меры по установлению сведений | п.1: "меры по установлению в отношении своих бенефициарных владельцев сведений" | Related material |

**Distinction from Article 7.1 p.3.1**:
- Article 7.1 p.3.1: Object was "сведения обо всех случаях документированного отказа в совершении нотариального действия" (multiple layers: cases + type of event + external reference)
- Article 6.1: Object is more straightforward "информация о своих бенефициарных владельцах" BUT becomes complex when combined with deadlines, scope, and multiple points

**Classification**: A (basic) / B (segmentation required)

**Result for H1**: CONFIRMED

## 7. SCOPE

### 7.1 Scope Definition

| Norm | Scope | Type | Evidence |
|------|-------|------|----------|
| п.1 | All legal entities (except per п.2) | Universal | "Юридическое лицо обязано" |
| п.2 | Exception applied | Restrictive | "не распространяется на лиц, указанных в ... статьи 7" |
| п.3.1 | All updates to beneficial owner information | Information-based | "обновлять информацию о своих бенефициарных владельцах" |
| п.3.2 | All information obtained and measures taken | Information-based | "информацию о своих бенефициарных владельцах и о принятых мерах" |
| п.4 | Information "necessary for beneficial owner determination" | Purpose-based | "информацию, необходимую для установления своих бенефициарных владельцев" |
| п.5 | All founders, participants, controlling entities | Role-based | "физические и юридические лица, являющиеся учредителями или участниками юридического лица или иным образом контролирующих его" |
| п.7.1 | Extended to foreign entities operating in RF | Territorial | "осуществляющие свою деятельность на территории Российской Федерации" |

### 7.2 SCOPE vs QUANTIFIER Distinction (HYPOTHESIS H6)

**RECALL: YES | APPLICATION: YES**

In Article 7.1 p.3.1, the issue was "сведения обо ВСЕХ случаях документированного отказа":
- "всех" is QUANTIFIER (how many: all)
- "документированного" is QUALIFIER (type: documented refusals)
- Confusion: is "всех" a quantifier or does it relate to scope?

**In Article 6.1**, we see more refined scope patterns:

```
Point 3.1: "регулярно, но не реже одного раза в год либо в случае изменения сведений"
           ↑ This is FREQUENCY (how often), not QUANTIFIER
           ↑ This is SCOPE LIMITER (when to update)

Point 3.2: "информацию о своих бенефициарных владельцах и о принятых мерах"
           ↑ "своих" = possessive scope (their own beneficial owners)
           
Point 4: "у физических и юридических лиц, являющихся учредителями или участниками"
         ↑ "учредителями или участниками" = role-based scope
```

**Classification**: A (scope clearly distinguished from quantity)

**Result for H6**: CONFIRMED (Scope and Quantifier are distinct concepts)

### 8.1 Quantitative Elements

|------------|-----------|------|----------|

### 8.2 QUANTIFIER vs QUALIFIER Distinction (HYPOTHESIS H2)

**RECALL: YES | APPLICATION: YES**

**Article 7.1 p.3.1 Error**: "документированного" was misclassified as QUANTIFIER (it is actually QUALIFIER)

**In Article 6.1**:

Test phrase: "хранить информацию о своих бенефициарных владельцах и о принятых мерах ... не менее пяти лет"

- **Quantifier**: "не менее пяти лет" (at least five years) → how long/how much
- **Qualifier**: "о принятых мерах" (about taken measures) → type of information

These are clearly separated. NO ERROR of misclassification here.

However, Point 8 contains: "владеет (имеет преобладающее участие более 25 процентов в капитале) юридическим лицом"

- **Quantifier**: "более 25 процентов" (more than 25 percent)
- **Qualifier**: "в капитале" (in capital) → what kind of percentage
- **Action-qualifier**: "владеет" vs "имеет возможность контролировать"

Again, CLEAR DISTINCTION. No confusion as in Article 7.1.

**Classification**: A (properly distinguished)

**Result for H2**: CONFIRMED (No errors; clear separation maintained throughout)

### 9.2 TRIGGER vs DEADLINE_START Distinction (HYPOTHESIS H5)

**RECALL: YES | APPLICATION: YES**

Unlike Article 7.1 p.3.1 which conflated trigger and deadline anchor:

**Article 6.1 Point 3.2** clearly separates them:
- **Temporal Anchor (Deadline Start)**: "со дня получения такой информации" (from the day information is obtained)
- **Duration**: "не менее пяти лет" (at least five years)
- This is NOT a trigger. It's the START POINT for a time period.

**Point 3.1** similarly separates:
- **Trigger**: "в случае изменения сведений" (IF information changes)
- **Frequency**: "не реже одного раза в год" (at least once per year)
- **These are distinct concepts**

**Point 6** shows request-based activation:
- **Trigger**: "по запросу уполномоченного органа" (upon request from authorized body)
- **Deadline**: "Порядок и сроки представления информации ... определяются Правительством" (procedure determined by Government)
- Clear separation

**Classification**: A (explicitly distinguished in text)

**Result for H5**: CONFIRMED + REFINED (Article 6.1 shows even clearer separation of trigger, frequency, and deadline anchor than Article 7.1)

- Condition: осуществляющие свою деятельность на территории РФ (conducting activities in RF territory)

**Scope Analysis**:
- Geographic criterion: деятельность на территории РФ
- NOT: регистрация на территории РФ (registration)
- NOT: официальный адрес в РФ (official address)
- Rather: actual operations/activities in RF

**Significance**:
- This is a SCOPE EXTENSION mechanism
- Applies existing rules to non-resident entities
- Creates obligation for foreign entities to comply with Article 6.1
- Requires foreign entity to:
  - Identify own beneficial owners
  - Provide information on request
  - Maintain records

**Classification**: A (explicitly stated)

**Observation**:
- Article 7.1 p.3.1 applies only to notaries (Russian professionals)
- Article 6.1 explicitly extends to foreign entities
- This is a new dimension: TERRITORIAL EXPANSION
- Pattern likely universal in RU anti-money laundering law (requires verification in other articles)

**Recommendation for Schema**:
- Add SCOPE_EXTENSION or TERRITORIAL_SCOPE field to capture:
  - Base scope (residents)
  - Extended scope (foreigners)
  - Condition for extension (activities in RF)
  - Type of foreign entity

## 23. SCHEMA_RECOMMENDATION

Based on FOUR experimental observations (Experiment 1-4), the following recommendations are proposed for ARCHITECTURE_CONTRACT v0.2:

### 23.1 Confirmed Universal Elements (No Change Required)

The following schema elements have been confirmed across multiple experiments and require no change:

1. **SUBJECT** — Confirmed universal
   - Current field adequate
   - Note: Article 6.1 reveals that SUBJECT can have multiple roles; recommend adding role metadata

2. **MODALITY** — Confirmed universal
   - Current field adequate
   - Types observed: obligation (обязан), permission (вправе), descriptive (определяется)

3. **ACTION** — Confirmed with refinement
   - Current field adequate
   - New capability: COMPOUND ACTION (multiple verbs with shared object/deadline)
   - Article 6.1 Point 3.1 shows this clearly

4. **OBJECT** — Confirmed with segmentation
   - Current field adequate
   - Refinement: allow nested structure to distinguish:
     - Object head (информация)
     - Qualifiers (о БВ)
     - Scope modifiers (своих)
     - Quantifiers (>25%)
   - NO new field required; internal structure refinement

5. **CONDITION** — Confirmed with distinction
   - Current field adequate
   - Clarification: CONDITION ≠ EXTERNAL_REFERENCE
   - Keep separate: exemption conditions vs. informational references

6. **EXTERNAL_REFERENCE** — Confirmed universal
   - Current field adequate
   - Refinement: distinguish reference types:
     - Information source (Article 7)
     - Exemption source (Article 7, Point 2)
     - Authority delegation (Government in Point 6)
     - Protective reference (Data Protection Law)

7. **DEADLINE** — Confirmed with multiplicity
   - Current field adequate
   - Refinement: Support MULTIPLE DEADLINES per norm
   - Article 6.1 shows annual update + 5-year storage + request-based timing (3 distinct deadlines)

8. **EXCEPTION** — Confirmed universal
   - Current field adequate
   - Article 6.1 Point 2 shows explicit exception
   - Article 6.1 Point 1 shows implicit exception (reasonable efforts)

### 23.2 New Fields Recommended for v0.2

Based on evidence from Article 6.1, add the following fields:

**Field 1: SUBJECT_ROLES** (Recommended)
- Type: Multi-valued attribute on SUBJECT
- Purpose: Distinguish roles played by different subjects
- Values: obligor, obligee, requestor, rule-setter, protected-party, exempt-party
- Justification: Article 6.1 demonstrates that one norm can involve 4-5 actors with different roles
- Example: 
  - ЮЛ: obligor + requestor (Point 4)
  - Учредители: obligor (Point 5) + protected (Point 5)
  - Authorities: requestor (Point 6)
  - Government: rule-setter (Point 6)
- Schema status: ADD to v0.2

**Field 2: DEFINITION** (Recommended)
- Type: Embedded definition
- Purpose: Capture term definitions within norms
- Structure: Term | Scope | Definitional elements
- Justification: Article 6.1 Point 8 provides essential definition; first clear example
- Example: бенефициарный владелец (>25% ownership OR control ability)
- Schema status: ADD to v0.2

**Field 3: DELEGATION** (Recommended)
- Type: Authority delegation specification
- Purpose: Track norm-setting power transferred to secondary authorities
- Structure: Delegated element | Delegatee | Authority level
- Justification: Article 6.1 Point 6 (second sentence) shows delegation of procedure/deadlines to Government
- Example: procedures and deadlines → Government
- Schema status: ADD to v0.2

**Field 4: SCOPE_EXTENSION** (Optional/Recommended)
- Type: Geographic or categorical scope extension
- Purpose: Track when rules extend beyond primary scope
- Structure: Base scope | Extended scope | Extension condition
- Justification: Article 6.1 Point 7.1 extends to foreign entities with condition (activity in RF)
- Example: Russian entities → Foreign entities (if active in RF)
- Schema status: ADD to v0.2 (optional but useful)

### 23.3 Fields Not Recommended for Addition

Based on meta-audit assessment:

**NOT Recommended: OBJECT_QUALIFIER (separate field)**
- Reason: Can be handled by nested structure in OBJECT field
- Complexity: Would create redundancy if made separate
- Evidence: Article 6.1 shows qualifiers naturally contained within object descriptions

**NOT Recommended: ACTION_CLUSTER (separate field)**
- Reason: Current ACTION field can accommodate compound actions with metadata
- Approach: Add compound_action flag and common_parameters tracking
- Evidence: Article 6.1 Point 3.1 handles this naturally without separate field

**NOT Recommended: TEMPORAL_ANCHOR (separate field)**
- Reason: DEADLINE_START already captures this
- Refinement: Enhance DEADLINE_START to distinguish:
  - Anchor type (receipt, event, calendar)
  - Anchor specification (text reference)
- Evidence: Article 6.1 Points 3.1-3.2 show clear anchors that fit existing field

### 23.4 Schema Evolution Path

**Minimal Change Option** (Conservative):
- Keep all existing fields
- Enhance internal documentation
- Note: supports Article 6.1 analysis without schema changes

**Recommended Change Option** (Balanced):
- Add SUBJECT_ROLES metadata
- Add DEFINITION field
- Enhance EXTERNAL_REFERENCE to track reference type
- Support MULTIPLE DEADLINES in DEADLINE field
- Justify: Enables capturing key Article 6.1 patterns

**Comprehensive Change Option** (Ambitious):
- Add all four new fields (SUBJECT_ROLES, DEFINITION, DELEGATION, SCOPE_EXTENSION)
- Restructure OBJECT field to support nesting
- Enhance DEADLINE to array of deadline objects
- Justify: Full capture of new patterns; supports future articles

**Recommendation**: Proceed with RECOMMENDED CHANGE OPTION for v0.2.
- Minimal disruption to existing analysis
- Captures all new Article 6.1 patterns
- Not overengineering for single example
- Preserves simplicity while enabling new capabilities
